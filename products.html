Yes! All our cocoa beans are 100% fair-trade certified, organic, and grown without artificial palm oil.

We use natural spirulina extract and white chocolate cocoa butter to create our signature pastel sky-blue dusting.

Orders are handcrafted fresh daily and shipped in temperature-controlled cloud boxes within 2-3 business days.
